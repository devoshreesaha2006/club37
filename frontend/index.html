ABOUT CLUB 37

Built by riders, for riders.

Club 37 started with a handful of riders who wanted more than solo miles — a crew to plan routes with, fix bikes with, and trust on the road. Today, membership is reviewed by hand so every rider who joins actually rides.

01

Real Rides

Weekend runs, long-haul tours, and late-night city loops — planned by members, for members.

02

Reviewed Membership

Every application is read by the Club 37 team before anyone is added to the roster. No bots, no spam accounts.

03

Shared Standards

Respect the road, respect the crew. Members look out for each other on and off the bike.

CLUB VALUES

What we ride for.

Four principles hold the club together, on the road and off it.

Discipline

Maintained bikes, planned routes, no reckless riding.

Loyalty

Members ride for each other, not just for themselves.

Craft

Respect for the machine — its mechanics, its history, its upkeep.

Belonging

A seat at the table for riders who show up and stay committed.
